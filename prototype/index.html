Sphatik OS

Shell prototype 0.1

A working sketch of the system shell: glass materials, spring motion and the gesture language from the design spec.

  • Swipe up on the lock screen to unlock.
  • Drag down from the top left for notifications, top right for Control Center.
  • Drag down anywhere on home to open the Intent Bar. Try “45 x 18”.
  • Swipe up from the bottom edge to leave an app.
  • Swipe up from the bottom and pause to see recent apps. Flick a card up to close it.
  • Start a timer in Clock, then leave the app. It keeps counting in the Halo.
  • Play music in Control Center, then tap the Halo.
  • All 16 stock apps are designed and interactive. Try calling someone in Phone, texting in Messages, or starting navigation in Maps.
  • Press the side button to lock. Hold it to restart and watch the boot screen.
  • Swipe left on home for the App Library. Terminal lives there under Developer.
  • Long-press a message, photo or note in an app and drag it onto the Halo to keep it on the Halo Shelf.
  • Glass Lens: two-finger long-press, Alt+L, or the Lens tile in Control Center.
  • Control Center also has Nearby, Lens and Desktop mode. Settings has Privacy, Battery and Language (including Hindi for the whole phone).
  • Ask the Intent Bar “What was the Wi-Fi password Aman sent?”
  • Long-press home to rearrange icons and add widgets, or long-press the lock screen to customise it.

Type is set in Outfit as a stand-in for Sphatik Sans.
