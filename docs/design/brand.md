# Sphatik brand guidelines

## Names

| Name | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Sphatik OS** | The operating system. Written with a capital S, never "SPHATIK" in running text |
| **Sphatik** (स्फटिक) | Short form once the context is clear. Meaning: crystal |
| **SKY** | The maker mark shown on the boot screen, above "SPHATIK OS" |
| **Halo**, **Intent Bar**, **App Library** | Feature names, capitalised as shown |

Do not use "Android" in product names or logos. "Runs Android apps" is fine as a description.

## The SKY mark

- Wordmark: S K Y in a thin geometric sans, wide letter-spacing (about 0.38 em), with no symbol or dot. On the boot screen it sits exactly in the centre of the display.
- White on black during boot; on light backgrounds use Ink (#16142A).
- Clear space around the mark: at least the height of the letters on every side.
- Never stretch, outline, add shadows, or place over busy photography.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | #16142A | Text on light surfaces, dark UI base |
| Crystal Cyan | #8AF0F0 | Accent gradient start, highlights |
| Amethyst | #A78BFA | Accent gradient end |
| Violet | #6546F2 | Links, active states on light surfaces |
| Rose Quartz | #FF8FB7 | Secondary accent, wallpapers |
| Mist | #F7F7FA | Light app surfaces |

The signature accent is the **Crystal Cyan to Amethyst** gradient at 135 degrees. Use it for one element per screen at most.

## Typography

Sphatik Sans for everything (Outfit stands in until it is finished). Display weights 200 to 250 for large numbers; 600 to 700 for titles; 400 for body.

## Wallpapers

Liquid Aurora (default), Obsidian, Quartz Dawn, Amethyst. Marketing images use Liquid Aurora or Obsidian.

## Voice

Calm, clear, confident, a little warm. We explain, we do not hype. Short sentences. Honest about limitations ("Banking apps may not work") rather than hiding them.
