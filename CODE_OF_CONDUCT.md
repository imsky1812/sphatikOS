# Code of Conduct

Sphatik is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. We want everyone to feel welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Help newcomers; everyone was new once.
- Respect people's time: be clear, be concise, follow up.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers at conduct@sphatik.dev (placeholder address; set before launch). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or remove anyone who breaks this code, in any project space.
